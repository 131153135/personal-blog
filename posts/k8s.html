第一份 Deployment 编排文件：YAML 字段逐项解释

YAML2026-10-08 · 编排实践

从 apiVersion、kind、metadata、spec 开始，逐行理解一份部署 Nginx 的 Deployment 配置，理解副本数、镜像、端口和标签选择器。

apiVersion: apps/v1
kind: Deployment
metadata:
  name: nginx-demo
spec:
  replicas: 2
  selector:
    matchLabels:
      app: nginx
  template:
    metadata:
      labels:
        app: nginx
    spec:
      containers:
      - name: nginx
        image: nginx
        ports:
        - containerPort: 80
